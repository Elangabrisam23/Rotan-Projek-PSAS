About Ridwan Rotan

Ridwan Rotan is a rattan export business based in Cirebon, West Java, a region known across Indonesia for rattan furniture. Since 2024 we have made furniture and handicrafts by hand and shipped them to buyers overseas.

Every piece is woven, bent and tied by local craftspeople using natural rattan cane. We now ship to Australia, and we built this site to answer the questions buyers ask us most: is it in stock, what sizes are there, what will it cost, and when will it arrive.
